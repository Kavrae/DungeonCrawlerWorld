# Boss mechanics

**Every boss should have a gimmick that makes it easier to defeat** -- something a player can notice, learn and turn
against it, so a fight rewards attention rather than only stats. A boss without one is just a large monster.

Ideas for future bosses, collected as they come up. Each names what it needs from the game and what the player does
about it.

## Moving a boss into its own attacks

The tiles a caster stands on when it releases an action are never affected by that action, but a caster that is
somewhere else when the action lands is (PLAN-targeting-model.md, "The caster's own tiles"). A boss whose attack takes
time to land -- a slow projectile, a lobbed shot, a long windup over an area -- can be caught in it if the player moves
the boss into the landing area before it lands: a pull, a push, a swap of places, a teleport, or luring it to follow.

- **What the player learns:** watch the telegraph of the boss's big attack, then drag or lure the boss into it.
- **Needs:** travel time on paths (PLAN-targeting-model.md, Phase 5), or windups that resolve on tiles while the caster
  is free to move; and player abilities that move another entity (push, pull, swap, teleport other).
- **Tuning:** the boss's own attack should hurt it noticeably -- that is the reward -- so its resistances should not
  cover its own signature damage type.
- **Rare for anyone else:** players and ordinary NPCs only meet this by accident; it should feel like a boss's weakness,
  not a common hazard.
